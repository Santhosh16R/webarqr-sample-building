FROM SCREEN TO SPACE

Three steps. One new view.

    Find your floor

    Open floor AR and aim at a clear, well-lit patch of floor. Move your phone slowly.

  2. 02

    Tap to build

    Tap the blue ring to place the tower. In a native viewer, use its floor-placement controls.

    Watch it rise

    Cranes lift, robotic arms work and drones fly as the tower rises. Replay the scene, or reset to choose another spot.

A little help getting started +

Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on an ARCore-supported Android phone. Aim at the actual floor; horizontal-surface tracking can also recognize tables.

Allow AR access when asked. Native viewers provide their own placement and playback controls. No marker is needed.
